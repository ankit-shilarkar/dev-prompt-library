Every step follows the same loop

AI is a pair programmer, not an answer key. The loop keeps the thinking yours and makes AI a multiplier instead of a crutch.

Attempt

Work the task on your own first: restate it, plan it, try it. Write down your approach, even if it's rough.

+25 XP try-first bonus

Pair

Use the step's linked prompts to get hints, a review, or a second approach. Compare, don't copy.

Reflect

Write what the AI did differently and what you'll do next time. This is where the learning sticks.

+25 XP reflection bonus
